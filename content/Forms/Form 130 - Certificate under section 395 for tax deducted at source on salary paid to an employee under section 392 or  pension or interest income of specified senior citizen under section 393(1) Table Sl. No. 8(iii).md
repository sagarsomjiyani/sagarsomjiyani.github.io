quarter ending on 31st March of the tax year shall contain the details of tax deducted and deposited for all the quarters of 
the tax year. 
7.  If an assessee is employed under more than one employer during the year, each of the employers shall issue Part 
A and Part B of the certificate in Form No. 130 pertaining to the period for which such assessee was employed with each 
of the employer. 
8.  Some of the information in the Form would be pre-filled to the extent possible. 
9.  Amounts to be filled in ₹ unless otherwise provided.
