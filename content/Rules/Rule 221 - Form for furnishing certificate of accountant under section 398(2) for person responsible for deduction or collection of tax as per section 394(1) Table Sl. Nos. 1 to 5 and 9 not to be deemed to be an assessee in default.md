(1) The certificate from an accountant, as defined in section 515(3)(b), under [[Section 398 - Consequences of Failure to Deduct or Pay or Collect or Pay|section 398(2)]] shall be furnished,—
(a)  in [[Form 149 - Form for furnishing accountant certificate under section 398(2) for person responsible for deduction of tax not to be deemed to be an assessee in default|Form No. 149]], for non-deduction or short deduction of tax; and
(b)  in [[Form 150 - Form for furnishing accountant certificate under section 398(2) for person responsible for collection of tax as per section 394(1) Table Sl. No. 1 to 5 and 9 not to be deemed to be an assessee in default|Form No. 150]], for non-collection or short collection of tax.

(2) Form No. 149 or Form No. 150 referred to in sub-rule (1) shall be furnished to the Director General of Income-tax (Systems) or the person authorised by him.
